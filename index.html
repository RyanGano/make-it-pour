How to Play

Cups slide along the floor. Move the bottle over one, pour, and keep the stream on target until the cup is full.

Controls

  • Touch: drag anywhere — the bottle follows your finger and pours while you hold.
  • Mouse: move to aim, hold the button to pour.
  • Keyboard: ← → ↑ ↓ to move, space to pour, P or esc to pause.

The Clock

  • A round starts at 60 seconds. Filling a cup adds 1 second back.
  • Keep pouring into a cup that is already full for a half-second bonus.
  • Every unfilled cup on screen drains the clock faster — the rate under the timer shows how much.

Scoring

  • Each filled cup is 100 points; golden cups are 300 and give double the time bonus.
  • Fills in quick succession build a combo multiplier up to x9.
  • Cups slide faster as the round goes on and as you fill more — a hot streak buys time and outruns you at once.

Your Privacy

This game collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, and no server that ever sees you play. Your best score is saved only in this browser, on this device, and never leaves it — clearing your browser data erases it for good. The page's one network request is re-fetching itself to notice when a new version has been deployed; it carries no information about you.
