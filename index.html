How to Play

Cups slide along the floor. Move the bottle over one, pour, and keep the stream on target until the cup is full.

Controls

  • Touch: drag anywhere — the bottle follows your finger and pours while you hold.
  • Mouse: move to aim, hold the button to pour.
  • Keyboard: ← → ↑ ↓ to move, space to pour, P or esc to pause.

Filling pays points

  • Each filled cup is 100 points, and a golden cup is 300.
  • Fills in quick succession build a combo multiplier up to x9, and the combo is what makes a score big.
  • Cups slide faster as the round goes on and as you fill more — a hot streak buys time and outruns you at once.

Over pouring pays tips

  • Keep the stream on a cup that is already full and it pays every third of a second. The first half second is free — drops still in the air are not your fault — and it only pays the clock.
  • Stay past that and every payout hands you tips — climbing one at a time, up to 2 a payout until Bigger Tips raises the cap — and knocks a point off your combo each time.
  • So every full cup is a choice: move on for the score, or lean on it for the money. You cannot have both from the same cup.
  • The exception: a golden cup tips you 8 just for finishing it.

The Clock

  • A round starts at 60 seconds. Over pouring is the only thing that gives time back, so you cannot ignore it completely.
  • Every unfilled cup on screen drains the clock faster — the rate under the timer shows how much.

Tips, rank and the back bar

  • Tips are the money. The Upgrades button on the end screen opens the back bar and spends them.
  • Your best round's score is your rank, and rank decides what the bar will sell you at all. Grind tips alone and you end up rich in front of a locked shelf; chase score alone and you can afford nothing.
  • What you buy carries into every later round, and the bar is saved in this browser — your tips, your stock, your rank and your best score are all still there when you come back.

Start Over

Wipes the bar back to opening night: no tips, no stock, rank 1, no best score. There is no undo.

Your Privacy

This game collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, and no server that ever sees you play. Your bar — tips, upgrades, rank and best score — is saved only in this browser, on this device, and never leaves it. Clearing your browser data erases it for good, and Reset Everything above does the same on purpose. The page's one network request is re-fetching itself to notice when a new version has been deployed; it carries no information about you.
